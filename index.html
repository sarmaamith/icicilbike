PRIVACY NOTICE

Last update: 07/09/2026

Welcome to the Privacy Notice of ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited (“Privacy Notice”). In this document, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited acts as the Data Fiduciary and remains committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Notice outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your Digital Personal Data. All Processing of Digital Personal Data is undertaken in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules made there under (“DPDPA”), and other applicable data protection laws and regulatory requirements relevant to ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited (“ICICI Lombard”).

Throughout this Privacy Notice, the terms “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to ICICI Lombard. For additional information about our company, please visit www.icicilombard.com.

“Personal Data,” also known as “Personal Information” or “personally identifiable information”, refers to any information or data relating to an individual that allows the respective individual to be identified. This encompasses a wide range of details, including your name, date of birth, and email address.

What This Privacy Notice Covers

This Privacy Notice applies to all Digital Personal Data that we collect, use, share, or otherwise process through our online and offline activities that display, link to, or reference this Privacy Notice.

This includes information collected through our websites, mobile applications, official social media pages, email communications, online and offline campaigns, branches, offices, customer service channels, and conversations or correspondence with our representatives.

In this Privacy Notice, our online activities are referred to as “Sites,” and our offline activities are referred to as “Services.” Our Sites may contain links to third-party websites, applications, or platforms that are not operated or controlled by us. Such third-party platforms are not covered by this Privacy Notice, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy notices.

Definitions

“Consent” means the consent given by a Data Principal that is free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous with a clear affirmative action, and signifies agreement to the Processing of her Digital Personal Data for the Specified Purpose.

“Data Fiduciary” means any person who alone or in conjunction with other persons determines the purpose and means of Processing Digital Personal Data. For this Privacy Notice, ICICI Lombard is the Data Fiduciary.

“Data Principal” means the individual to whom the Digital Personal Data relates and, where such individual is a child, includes the parents or lawful guardian of such child, and where such individual is a person with disability, includes her lawful guardian acting on her behalf.

“Data Processor” means any person who processes Digital Personal Data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary.

“Digital Personal Data” means Personal Data in digital form.

“Digital Platforms” means our websites, mobile applications, portals, online services and other digital channels through which we provide information, products, services or customer support.

“Personal Data” means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data. References to “Personal Information” in this Privacy Notice shall be construed accordingly.

“Processing”, in relation to Digital Personal Data, means a wholly or partly automated operation or set of operations performed on Digital Personal Data, and includes operations such as collection, recording, organisation, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, alignment or combination, indexing, sharing, disclosure by transmission, dissemination or otherwise making available, restriction, erasure or destruction.

“Services” means the insurance products, assistance, policy servicing, claims and other services offered or facilitated by ICICI Lombard.

“Data Protection Law(s)” refers to the entirety of all laws and regulations applicable to the Processing of Personal Data, i.e. the India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA), and the rules and amendments made thereunder from time to time.

All capitalized terms used in this Privacy Notice and not otherwise defined herein, unless the context otherwise requires, have the meanings given to such terms under Data Protection Laws.

Your Consent

We collect, use, disclose, or otherwise process your Digital Personal Data only when such Processing is fair, lawful, and necessary. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our the designated privacy team using the contact details provided in this Privacy Notice. If you choose not to provide certain Digital Personal Data, we may be unable to deliver the products or services you have requested.

How We Collect Your Digital Personal Data

We collect Digital Personal Data through the following sources and channels:

  • Directly from you: when you request a quotation, complete a proposal form, purchase or renew a policy, create or use an online account, make a payment, submit a claim, request a service, participate in a survey, raise an enquiry or grievance, communicate with us, or otherwise provide information through physical or digital channels.
  • Through our Digital Platforms and technologies: when you use our websites, mobile applications or other online services, including through cookies, pixels, web beacons, software development kits, device permissions, logs, analytics tools and similar technologies.
  • From insurance intermediaries and distribution channels: including agents, brokers, corporate agents, banks, web aggregators, group policyholder, MISP, POSP and other authorized channels such as Bima Sugam, through which you seek or receive insurance products or services.
  • From service providers involved in insurance delivery: including hospitals, doctors, diagnostic centres, third-party administrators, wellness providers, garages, repairers, surveyors, loss assessors, investigators, roadside-assistance providers, call centres, payment service providers and other vendors assisting with underwriting, servicing or claims.
  • From third parties and authorized databases: including reinsurers, co-insurers, insurance repositories, the Insurance Information Bureau, credit bureaus, KYC or CKYC agencies, VAHAAN, SARATHI, fraud-monitoring networks, government or regulatory portals, law-enforcement authorities, courts, tribunals, publicly available sources and other sources permitted by law.

Information may be collected in physical or digital form and may subsequently be digitized, verified, combined or updated using information received from the sources described above. We collect only such Digital Personal Data as is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Notice, the relevant consent form, or applicable product and service documentation.

What Digital Personal Data We Collect About You

Identity, Personal and Contact Information

We may collect your name, residential and correspondence address, state and city, email address, telephone or mobile number, signature, date of birth, age, gender, occupation or vocation, marital and family status, nationality, citizenship, residency or domicile, educational background, employment history, skills and experience, professional licenses and affiliations, physical attributes, photographs, video recordings, facial scan, and relationship details. We may also collect nominee, beneficiary, dependent, family member, joint applicant, authorized representative, and guardian details, including their name, age, relationship, contact details, and supporting documents.

Government and Regulatory Identifiers

We may collect Aadhaar details or proof of possession of Aadhaar, Permanent Account Number (PAN), driving licence number and driving records, passport and visa details, voter ID, ABHA ID, DARPAN ID, CKYCR number, GSTIN, Electronic Insurance Account (eIA) number, vehicle registration details, and other officially valid documents or identifiers issued by government bodies, regulators, insurance repositories, or authorized agencies. This may include KYC documents relating to proposers, policyholders, insured persons, claimants, nominees, and beneficiaries.

Financial, Payment and Employment Information

We may collect payment details, bank account and other financial account details, transaction and payment records, premium and refund information, credit history and credit score, assets, income, tax information, employment or business details, salary slips, bank statements, income tax returns, employer information, and other financial information required for underwriting, premium collection, refunds, payouts, recoveries, claims, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance.

Health and Medical Data

Where relevant to a health, travel, personal accident, motor, liability or other insurance product, underwriting decision, assistance service or claim, we may process information about your physical or mental health and medical history. This may include existing or previous illnesses, injuries, disabilities, symptoms, treatments, procedures, surgeries, medication, family medical history, allergies, maternity or pregnancy-related information where relevant, height, weight, body mass index, tobacco or alcohol use, occupation-related health risks, fitness or wellness information, and information provided in health declarations or questionnaires. We may also process pre-policy medical examination results, diagnostic and laboratory reports, prescriptions, discharge summaries, hospitalization and treatment records, medical certificates, hospital bills, doctor or healthcare-provider details, ABHA-related information where provided or permitted, cashless authorization records, and other information necessary for medical underwriting, policy servicing, assistance, claim assessment, investigation or settlement.

Other Insurance-Related Data

Depending on the insurance product or service you request, we may process information about the subject matter of insurance and the associated risk. This may include:

  • Motor insurance data: vehicle registration, make, model, variant, fuel type, chassis and engine details, age, usage, location, ownership, finance or hypothecation details, driving licence and driving history, odometer readings, inspection photographs or videos, telematics where applicable, previous policy, no-claim bonus, accident, repair and claim history.
  • Property and engineering insurance data: property address and location, ownership or interest, occupancy, construction, age, value, use, security and fire-protection arrangements, machinery or equipment details, site plans, photographs, inspection and risk-engineering reports, natural-catastrophe exposure and prior loss history.
  • Travel insurance data: passport and visa details, nationality and residency, travel dates, destinations, itinerary, ticket and booking details, purpose of travel, accompanying travelers, trip disruption or baggage information, emergency contacts and assistance records.
  • Marine, cargo and transit insurance data: vessel, shipment, cargo, packaging, invoice, route, origin, destination, transporter, consignment, bill of lading or airway bill, storage, transit, loss, inspection and recovery information.
  • Crop, cattle and other agricultural insurance data: farmer or beneficiary identifiers, land or farm location and records, crop, season, acreage, sowing and yield information, livestock identity, breed, age, health and valuation, weather or parametric data, enrolment details and information received through government or authorized scheme portals.
  • Commercial, liability and specialized insurance data: business activities, premises, turnover, workforce, professional qualifications, licences, contracts, assets, customers, vendors, cybersecurity posture, incidents, liabilities, exposures, insured persons and other information needed to assess or service commercial, professional indemnity, cyber, aviation, group, gadget, warranty or other specialized insurance risks.
  • Policy and claims data: quotations, proposals, declarations, policy numbers, coverage, premium, endorsements, renewals, cancellations, insured persons, nominees or beneficiaries, prior insurance, claims, accidents, incidents, loss circumstances, photographs, videos, police or legal records, estimates, invoices, surveys, investigations, recoveries, salvage and settlement information.
Digital Platform, Device and Usage Information

When you access or use our websites, mobile applications or other Digital Platforms, we may collect the date and time of access, IP address, device type, operating system, browser type, device identifiers, approximate location, pages viewed, links clicked, referring webpage, session and activity records, and performance and diagnostic data, including how you interact with our platforms.

Where you enable them and subject to permissions, we may process biometric authentication data, camera or photo access, location and device-based identifiers, and we process passwords, PINs and login credentials in secure or encrypted form.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings; for details of the cookies we use and their purposes, please refer to our Cookie Notice.

This privacy notice applies to all information provided to us or obtained by us through your use of our digital platforms and services. It does not apply to websites controlled by third parties that are not affiliated with us, including our Group Companies (“Third Party Sites”).

How We Retain Your Digital Personal Data

We retain your Digital Personal Data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, deliver products and services, comply with legal and regulatory obligations, maintain accurate business and financial records, and address queries, claims, disputes, investigations, or proceedings. When the purpose has been fulfilled and retention is no longer required, we securely delete or erase the data, including copies held by authorized processors, in accordance with applicable requirements.

How We Keep Your Digital Personal Data Secure

We implement reasonable physical, technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards to protect Digital Personal Data against loss, theft, unauthorized access, disclosure, copying, use, alteration, or destruction. These safeguards include access controls, encryption, masking, monitoring, incident management, and contractual protections for Data Processors. In the event of a Digital Personal Data breach affecting you, we will investigate, mitigate, remediate, and provide notifications to affected Data Principals and the Data Protection Board as required by applicable law.

Children and Persons with Disabilities

Children under 18 years of age and persons with disabilities requiring a lawful guardian should not directly submit Digital Personal Data through our Sites. Their Digital Personal Data will be processed only pursuant to verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian, in accordance with applicable law. If you believe such information has been shared in error, please contact Customer Support at customersupport@icicilombard.com.

International Transfers of Digital Personal Data

Your Digital Personal Data may be transferred or processed outside India only where permitted under applicable Indian data protection laws, IRDAI requirements, and other applicable laws. We will not transfer Digital Personal Data for Processing to any country or territory to which such transfer has been restricted or prohibited by the Central Government under the DPDPA, including any country or territory notified for this purpose from time to time. For all permitted international transfers, we implement appropriate contractual, technical, organizational, and security safeguards to protect your Digital Personal Data, regardless of the location in which it is stored or processed.

Your Responsibilities as a Data Principal

You must comply with applicable law while exercising your rights; not impersonate another person or suppress material information; not submit false or frivolous grievances or complaints; and provide only verifiably authentic information when seeking correction or erasure. You are also responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of passwords, PINs, and credentials used to access our Sites and Services.

Updates to This Privacy Notice

We may revise this Privacy Notice periodically. Whenever updates are made, we will update the “Last update” date at the top of this Notice. If significant changes materially affect our privacy practices or the purpose of Processing, we may provide advance notice through appropriate means and seek fresh consent where required.
